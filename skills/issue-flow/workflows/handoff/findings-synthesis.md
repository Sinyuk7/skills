# findings-synthesis

Purpose:

- turn curated evidence into structured findings
- separate facts, inferences, conflicts, and open questions

Expected outputs:

- `analysis/evidence-pack.xml`
- `analysis/findings.xml`
- `analysis/code-map.yaml`

Rules:

- evidence references should point to curated materials
- findings should stay traceable and reversible
- unresolved ambiguity should remain explicit
